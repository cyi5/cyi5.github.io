Competitive intelligence monitor

Live · twice daily
Problem
A fast-moving genomics and real-world data market, and no way to follow it in near real time without aggregating news by hand.
What it does
Feeds 19 structured Google Alerts queries to Claude, which checks each item for relevance, summarizes by competitor and key account, and posts to Slack twice a day. Loose or wrong matches are flagged for a human to review.
